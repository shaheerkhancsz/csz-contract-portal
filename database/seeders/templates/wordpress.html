Services Covered

During the term CSZ will provide the following services for the Website.

Updates

  • WordPress core updates (security and stable releases), tested after each update.
  • Updates of installed themes and plugins[[if client_licences]], where the Client holds a valid licence for paid ones[[endif]].
  • Fixing problems on the Website caused directly by an update CSZ carried out.

Backups and Restore

  • {{backup_frequency}} automated backups of the Website files and database, kept for at least {{backup_retention_days}} days.
  • A fresh backup before every major update.
  • Restoring the Website from the latest good backup if it breaks or goes down.
[[if security]]

Security

  • Security plugin set-up and monitoring, including login protection.
  • Regular malware scans[[if malware_removal]], and removal of malware found during the term[[endif]].
  • Checking that the SSL certificate (HTTPS) stays valid.
[[endif]] [[if uptime_monitoring]]

Monitoring and Performance

  • Uptime monitoring, with action taken when the Website goes down.
  • A monthly health check: database clean-up, cache check and basic speed tuning.
  • Checking that contact and enquiry forms are working.
[[endif]]

Minor Changes and Support

  • Small content changes (text, images, prices, contact details, adding a post) of up to {{content_hours}} hours per month. Unused hours do not carry over.

Services Not Covered

The following are not part of this Agreement. CSZ can provide them for an extra charge after a written quotation:

  • Website redesign, new pages, sections or layouts.
  • New features, custom coding, or new plugin or theme development.
  • Content writing, translation, graphic design, photo or video editing.
  • SEO, online advertising, social media management and other digital marketing.
  • Setting up e-commerce, payment gateways or third-party integrations.
  • Content changes beyond {{content_hours}} hours per month.
  • Purchase or renewal of paid plugins, themes, licences, domain names or hosting.
  • Email account set-up or email delivery problems.
  • Moving the Website to a new server or host.
  • Fixing damage caused by the Client, its staff or any third party who changed the Website, server or settings without CSZ's knowledge.
  • Problems caused by the hosting provider, internet service or other third-party services outside CSZ's control.
  • Training of the Client's staff.

Client Responsibilities

The Client will:

  • Give CSZ administrator access to WordPress and, where needed, to the hosting control panel.
  • Tell CSZ before making changes to the Website or installing plugins or themes.
  • Supply content (text, images, files) in final form and on time.
  • Keep licences for paid plugins and themes, and its hosting and domain services, active.
  • Keep its own passwords safe and tell CSZ straight away about any suspected security problem.

Ownership

The Client owns the Website content, data and domain name. Nothing in this Agreement transfers that ownership to CSZ.
