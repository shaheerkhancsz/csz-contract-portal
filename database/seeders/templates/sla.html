Purpose

CSZ developed or supports the Application for the Client: {{application_description}}. This Service Level Agreement sets out how CSZ will support, maintain and host the Application, and the response times it commits to.

@term

Services Covered

  • Fixing defects in the Application's code delivered by CSZ.
  • Security patches to the Application's framework and libraries.
  • Monitoring of availability and errors, with action taken on alerts.
  • [[if hosting]]Hosting of the Application on CSZ's servers, including daily backups kept for 30 days.[[endif]]
  • Up to {{change_hours}} hours per month of small changes and improvements requested by the Client. Unused hours do not carry over.
  • Support by email, the CSZ client portal and phone for Priority 1 issues.

Support Hours and Priorities

Support hours are {{support_hours}}.[[if after_hours]] Priority 1 issues are handled 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.[[endif]]

Availability

CSZ aims to keep the Application available {{uptime_percent}} of each calendar month, not counting planned maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead[[if not hosting]], failures of hosting or services provided by the Client or third parties[[endif]], or events outside CSZ's reasonable control.

[[if service_credits]]

If availability in a month falls below the commitment because of CSZ, the Client may claim a credit of 5% of that month's fee for each full 0.5% below the commitment, up to 25% of the month's fee. Credits must be claimed within 30 days and are the Client's only remedy for downtime.

[[endif]]

Change Requests

Work beyond the included hours, and new features, are quoted in writing and charged at {{change_rate}} per hour, or at a fixed price agreed in advance.

Services Not Covered

  • New modules or major redesigns.
  • Problems caused by changes made by the Client or third parties without CSZ's agreement.
  • Third-party services, licences and subscriptions used by the Application, which the Client pays for.
  • Data entry, training and content work.
@fees

Client Responsibilities

  • Name a contact person who can report issues and approve changes.
  • Report issues with enough detail for CSZ to reproduce them.
  • Keep third-party licences and services used by the Application active.
  • Test and approve changes in the staging environment before they go live, where one is provided.

Ownership

The Client owns its data. Ownership of the Application's source code is as set out in the development agreement between the Parties; this Agreement does not change it.
